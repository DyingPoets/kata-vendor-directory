# Vendor Directory — Feature Wishlist
**Source:** Alex Kim, Facilities Manager
**Captured:** [Session date]

## Must Have
1. Search vendors by name, category, and contact person
2. Store vendor name, category (12 categories), primary contact name/email/phone
3. Allow multiple contacts per vendor (e.g., billing vs. technical)
4. Mark vendors as active or inactive
5. Admin-only edit; read access for all staff

## Should Have
6. Bulk export to CSV
7. Track when contact info was last updated
8. Notes field per vendor (free text)

## Nice to Have
9. Vendor rating (internal, 1–5 stars)
10. Link to vendor contracts (document URL)
11. Auto-alert when a vendor contact hasn't been verified in 12 months
12. Integration with PO system (future phase)

## Explicitly Out of Scope
- Invoice processing
- Contract management
- Payment tracking
